# ControlTower | Synthetic Evidence-Metadata Automation

**Portfolio case study | Jamal Davison**

> **Evidence boundary:** The 12 records and resulting flags are synthetic. No live source integrations, real audit evidence, control-effectiveness testing, or compliance determination.

## 1. Scenario
Manual audit preparation makes it difficult to notice incomplete or overdue evidence records consistently.

## 2. My role and contribution
Built a Python pipeline and supporting fictional sample data to flag evidence-metadata exceptions and produce reviewable reports.

## 3. What I produced
- [Python validator](src/controltower.py)
- [Unit tests](tests/test_controltower.py)
- [Sample dashboard](outputs/dashboard.md)
- [Excel dashboard](ControlTower_Dashboard.xlsx)
- [Security and limitations](docs/SECURITY_AND_LIMITATIONS.md)

## 4. Decisions and reasoning
Designed deterministic rule-based checks with a human-review handoff, rather than equating complete metadata with effective security controls.

## 5. Results and verification
The linked deliverables are available for review in this repository. The 12 records and resulting flags are synthetic. No live source integrations, real audit evidence, control-effectiveness testing, or compliance determination.

## 6. What I would do next
With real organizational authorization, validate scope and assumptions with stakeholders, collect actual evidence, run appropriate tests, document exceptions, and seek accountable approval before any production rollout or closure claim.

## 7. Skills demonstrated
Security governance, risk assessment, evidence traceability, clear documentation, stakeholder-ready reporting, and separating proposed controls from verified results.
