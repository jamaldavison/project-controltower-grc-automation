# G02 — Enterprise Cybersecurity Risk Register

This is a **synthetic, educational portfolio exercise**, not an assessment of a real organization.

The full Excel workbook `ControlTower_GRC_Risk_Register.xlsx` contains six sheets: Executive Summary, Risk Register, Scoring Methodology, Treatment Log, Approval Log and Read Me.

## Scope
Ten simulated cybersecurity risks covering contractor offboarding, MFA, patching, production changes, audit logging, vendor risk, sensitive data, backups, access permissions and phishing. Each has inherent and illustrative residual scores, accountable owners and proposed treatments.

## Method
Likelihood (1–5) × Impact (1–5) = Risk Score (1–25). Low 1–7, Medium 8–14, High 15–25. Residual scores are **estimates**, not evidence of tested effectiveness. Risk acceptance entries are illustrative and are not actual company approvals.

## Case example — R001
Former contractors retain active accounts after incomplete offboarding notification. Inherent: 4 × 4 = 16 (High). Proposed treatment: remove unauthorized access, strengthen manager/HR offboarding, and perform monthly inactivity reviews. Illustrative target residual: 2 × 4 = 8 (Medium), subject to control testing and authorized review.

## Portfolio skills demonstrated
Risk identification; prioritization based on business criticality; inherent vs residual scoring; treatment plans; assigned risk/control owners; testing evidence; risk acceptance governance.
